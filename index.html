Client discovery brief

Let's get your website right from the start

This should take about five minutes. There's no login and nothing saves automatically, so it's best to finish it in one sitting. If you're unsure about any section, feel free to leave it blank. At the end you'll download your answers as a file (or copy them) to send back.

Section 01

About you & your business

The basics — who you are and who you help.

Do you already have a website?

Section 02

Goals for this website

What should this site actually accomplish for you?

What do you want this website to do for you? (pick all that apply)

Section 03

Services & offerings

This tells me which features the site actually needs — booking, a shop, gated content, or none of that.

Do you sell physical products (supplements, cookbooks, merchandise)?
Do you sell digital products (meal plans, PDFs, courses, eBooks)?
Do you offer a paid membership, subscription, or recurring program?
Will you be publishing content regularly (recipes, articles, videos)?
Do clients need to log in to see anything private (their plan, progress, files)?
Do you want an email newsletter signup on the site?

Section 04

Content you have

What already exists, and what we'll need to create together.

Which pages do you think you'll need? (pick all that apply)
Do you already have written copy for these pages, or would you like help writing it?
Do you have professional photos and a logo ready to use?
Do you have testimonials, reviews, or success stories to feature?

Section 05

Design & branding

Getting a feel for the look, tone, and any existing brand you already have.

Do you have existing brand guidelines (colors, fonts, a logo file)?
How do you want the site to feel? (pick all that apply)

Section 06

Technical details

The practical groundwork — domain, hosting, and any tools already in use.

Do you already own a domain name?
Do you have hosting in place, or will you need that set up?
After launch, do you want to be able to edit content yourself (text, photos, blog posts)?
Any tools you already use that the site should connect to?

Section 07

Trust & compliance

A few things specific to nutrition-related sites that are easy to forget until launch day.

Should the site include a medical or nutrition disclaimer?
Do you need a privacy policy and terms of service?

Section 08

Budget & timeline

Helps me scope something realistic for where you are right now.

Section 09

Anything else

Anything about your business or your vision for this site that the questions above didn't cover.
